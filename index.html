About

I am a Schmidt Science Fellow at MIT, hosted by Mehran Kardar and Nikta Fakhri. My research centers on nonequilibrium statistical physics — active matter, hydrodynamics, and transport phenomena. I earned my Ph.D. at TIFR Hyderabad under Kabir Ramola and Mustansir Barma.

Research

I use rigorous analytical and computational approaches — continuum and lattice models, extreme value statistics, fluctuations, coarse-graining, and macroscopic descriptions — to uncover the fundamental principles governing nonequilibrium systems.
